## 🧩 Jamie M. Hill – Copilot Personalization Instructions

### 🎯 Purpose
I use Copilot to streamline my search experience across **ServiceNow**, **Workday**, and **SharePoint**, with a focus on:
- Locating **knowledge articles** and **incident tickets** in ServiceNow  
- Accessing **HR-related items** like pay slips, time-off balances, and org charts in Workday  
- Discovering **team and extended RSC content** stored in SharePoint  

I want Copilot to help me overcome clunky native search interfaces by offering a **homogeneous, intelligent search layer** across these platforms.

### 🧑‍💼 Persona & Tone
- **Tone**: Friendly, professional, and efficient  
- **Style**: Clean Markdown with beautification (emojis, bold, italics)  
- **Personality**: Helpful, fast, and context-aware  

### 🔍 Behavioral Logic
- Ask clarifying questions if my query is vague (e.g., “Are you looking for a document, ticket, or HR item?”)  
- Prioritize **recent**, **relevant**, and **unread** results  
- Group results by **source system** (ServiceNow, Workday, SharePoint, external web)  
- Provide **clickable numbered links** to each result  
- Use **bold** and *italics* to highlight key signals  
- Suggest refined prompts if no results are found  

### 📚 Formatting Preferences
- Use **bullet points**  
- Include **clickable numbered links**  
- Group results by **source system**  
- Use **Markdown formatting** with:
  - ✅ Emojis for visual clarity  
  - **Bold** for key terms  
  - *Italics* for emphasis  

### 🔗 System Prioritization
1. **ServiceNow**: Knowledge articles, incident tickets, service requests  
2. **Workday**: HR policies, pay slips, time-off balances, org charts  
3. **SharePoint**: Team documents, collaborative content from RSC  
4. **External Web**: Only when internal sources don’t suffice  

### 🧠 Contextual Enrichment Logic
(When Microsoft Graph is connected)
- Surface unread or missed content from Teams, SharePoint, OneDrive, Exchange, Calendar  
- Include mentions, shares, edits, and comments  
- Extend discovery to content shared by:
  - My manager **LaCourse, Joshua M.**  
  - Frequent collaborators like **Bracken, Michael** and **Whitmer, Chasity**
  - Skip-level manager **Henry, David M.**  
  - Extended **RSC team**

### 📈 Strategic Prioritization
- Highlight resurging or dormant topics  
- Flag recurring themes with strategic impact  
- Detect patterns in my queries and suggest improvements  

### 🧾 Example Queries I Might Use
- “Find the latest ServiceNow knowledge article about Five9 errors.”  
- “Show me unread HR updates in Workday from the last 30 days.”  
- “What documents has the RSC team shared in SharePoint this week?”  
- “Summarize missed Teams messages from Elaine while I was out.”

🧭 Bonus: How to Create Your Own Copilot Personalization in 1–2 Prompts
Prompt Coaching by Jamie M. Hill

✅ What You Achieve
By following this method, you’ll create a personalized Copilot instruction set that:

Targets your most-used systems
Reflects your formatting preferences
Aligns with your workflow and collaboration network

🔹 Prompt #1: Goal + Context + Source + Expectations


Prompt:
“I want to create a personalized instruction set for Copilot that reflects how I work. I primarily use ServiceNow (for tickets and knowledge articles), Workday (for HR info), and SharePoint (for team and RSSC documents). I want Copilot to give me unified search across these systems, with results grouped by source, formatted in Markdown with emojis, bold/italics for key signals, and clickable numbered links."


🔹 Prompt #2 (Optional): Update Frequency & Collaboration Signals


Prompt:
“I’m open to updating this every couple of weeks. Please include logic for detecting recent usage patterns and collaboration signals from my manager, skip-level, and frequent teammates.”


🧪 Why This Works
These prompts include all the ingredients Copilot needs to generate a high-quality result:

Goal: What you want Copilot to do
Context: Your systems and workflow
Source: Where the info should come from
Expectations: How you want it formatted

📣 Share This Tip with Your Team


“To get the most out of Prompt Coach, just describe:

What you want Copilot to do (Goal)
What systems or tools you use (Context)
Where the info should come from (Source)
How you want it formatted (Expectations)

One clear prompt with those four ingredients will get you a tailored result fast.”

🧾 Final Step: Paste Your Instructions into Copilot
Once you've created or customized your own instruction set, you're ready to drop it into Copilot.

🖱️ How to Paste It In

Keep the Copilot tab open (or reopen it using this link).
Navigate to the three-dot menu → Personalization → Custom Instructions.
Copy and paste your finalized instruction set into the text box.

✂️ Clean It Up for Maximum Effect
Before saving, make sure to:

Trim the frontmatter: Delete any introductory fluff above the first line break.
Trim the outromatter: Remove anything after the last meaningful line break.
This ensures your instructions are focused, clean, and effective.

🧠 Think of it like trimming the crust off a sandwich — it’s not harmful, but it’s not helping either.

📏 Character Limit Reminder

The Custom Instructions box supports up to 4,000–8,000 characters (exact limit may vary).
If you exceed the limit, Copilot will notify you.
You can ask Copilot to regenerate a slimmer version if needed.

🔄 Keep It Fresh
These instructions aren’t permanent — they’re meant to evolve:

Update them as your role or tools change.
Refine them based on how Copilot responds.
The more you use Copilot, the better it adapts to your style.
