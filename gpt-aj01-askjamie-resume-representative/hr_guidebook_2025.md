# Human Resources Guidebook 2025 (RAG Edition)

## 1. Workforce Psychology & Sociology (2025)
- Employees expect flexibility, autonomy, psychological safety, and transparent communication.
- Hybrid work normalized; ~70% prefer remote-flex arrangements.
- Motivation trends: purpose-driven work, reduced tolerance for toxic leadership.
- Organizational behavior shifts emphasize distributed leadership and asynchronous communication.

## 2. Employment Types
### Salary
- Fixed annual pay; exempt from overtime.
### Hourly
- Paid per hour; entitled to overtime.
### Commission
- Performance-based; variable income.
### Union
- Governed by collective bargaining agreements.
### Non-Union
- Governed by employer policies.

## 3. Behavioral Expectations
### Explicit
- Ethics, attendance, safety, anti-harassment.
### Implicit
- Responsiveness, adaptability, initiative, willingness to use AI tools.

## 4. Interview Question Taxonomy
### Behavioral
- “Tell me about a time you resolved a conflict.”
### Technical
- Skills assessments.
### Situational
- Hypothetical scenarios.
### Culture/Values
- Ambiguity tolerance, communication style.

## 5. Internal vs. External Candidates
### Internal
- Focus on promotability, readiness, past performance.
### External
- Skill validation, cultural fit, adaptability.

## 6. Reviews, Promotions & Merit Cycles
- Modern trend: continuous-feedback models.
- Promotion requires consistent next-level performance.

## 7. Projections 2026–2030
- AI-driven skills-first hiring.
- Industry-aligned competency taxonomies.
- Behavioral analytics become normal.
- Increased use of AI for compensation modeling.

## 8. Organizational Behavior Trends
- Enterprise knowledge graphs.
- Flatter structures.
- Updated psychological contract: fairness, growth, purpose.
